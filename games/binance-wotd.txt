<h2><strong>What Is Binance Word of the Day (WOTD)?</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Word of the Day (WOTD) is a built-in puzzle inside the Binance app, similar in spirit to Wordle but themed around crypto vocabulary. Each week Binance sets a new subject — things like on-chain trading, staking, or a specific product line — and hands out a hidden word tied to that theme. Different accounts are shown different word lengths, so two friends playing on the same day might be solving a 4-letter word and a 7-letter word respectively.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The game isn't just a giveaway gimmick — it's built to nudge users toward learning terms they'd actually run into while trading or exploring Web3, and it rewards consistency rather than luck.</span></p>
